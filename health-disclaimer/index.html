Health disclaimer for the Satis calorie app

Last updated 22 November 2026

Join the betaRead the page

In short. Satis gives general information about food and weight. Talk to a doctor first if you are pregnant, under 18, have diabetes, take weight-loss medicine or have had an eating disorder. If counting starts to feel bad, stop; the support lines are below.

Satis, its guides and its calculators give general information about food, calories and body weight. They are not medical advice, and they don't replace a doctor, a registered dietitian or other qualified professional who knows your health.

Talk to a doctor before you start if

  • you're pregnant or breastfeeding;
  • you're under 18 (Satis is for adults);
  • you have diabetes, kidney disease, heart disease or another long-term condition, or take medicine that eating less could affect;
  • you take a weight-loss medicine such as semaglutide or tirzepatide;
  • you have had an eating disorder, or food and weight feel hard for you right now.

How Satis keeps targets safe

  • Daily targets don't go below 1,200 kcal for the female formula or 1,500 kcal for the male formula, the usual floors without medical supervision, or below your estimated resting burn.
  • Planned loss is capped at 1% of your body weight a week.
  • If your weight is already in a lean range, Satis suggests keeping it steady.

These limits are cautious general rules. They can't account for your medical history.

Numbers are estimates

Calories in real food vary with the recipe, the cook, the oil and the portion. Calorie formulas are averages and can be off by a few hundred calories for any one person. Satis adjusts your target from your weight trend, but the trend can also be affected by water, illness, medicines and hormones.

If food feels hard

If counting makes you anxious, or you notice you're restricting, bingeing or feeling guilty about food, it's fine to stop. Satis can be used without calorie numbers, and you can talk to someone:

  • India: Tele-MANAS, the government's free mental health helpline, on 14416.
  • UK: Beat, the eating disorder charity.
  • US: ANAD (National Association of Anorexia Nervosa and Associated Disorders).
  • Anywhere: your doctor. In an emergency, your local emergency number.
