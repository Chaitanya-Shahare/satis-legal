What Satis is

Satis is a tool for logging food and weight and for estimating calories. It gives general information. It is not a medical device and doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Read the health disclaimer before you use it to change how you eat.

Who can use it

You must be 18 or older. If you're pregnant or breastfeeding, have diabetes, take weight-loss medicine, or have had an eating disorder, check with a doctor first.

The beta

The beta is a test version distributed through Apple's TestFlight. It may have bugs, may change or stop working, and may lose data, so don't rely on it as your only record. Beta access is free and we can end it at any time. Feedback you send us can be used to improve Satis without any obligation to you.

Subscriptions (after launch)

When Satis launches on the App Store it will offer paid subscriptions. They're sold and billed by Apple, renew automatically until you cancel, and can be cancelled in your iPhone's Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions at least 24 hours before renewal. Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies. Prices are shown in the App Store before you buy. Apple's standard licence agreement applies to the app in addition to these terms.

Food data

Satis's numbers come from USDA FoodData Central (public domain), the Indian Nutrient Databank (Vijayakumar et al. 2024), Satis's own curated values, and, for barcodes, Open Food Facts (data under the Open Database Licence), USDA FoodData Central Branded Foods, and labels other Satis users read and shared (marked "Community"; checked only for impossible values, not verified by us). Real food varies by recipe, cook and portion, so every number is an estimate. We work to keep values accurate (see how we count) but can't guarantee them. If a value looks wrong, tell us.

Your logs, recipes and photos are yours. They're stored on your devices and in your iCloud, as described in the privacy policy.
